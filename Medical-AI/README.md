# Medical AI

Research resources for AI in medical and biomedical applications.

## Datasets

### [Rabbit Brain Histopathology Dataset](Rabbit-Brain-Histopathology-Dataset/)

H&E- and Nissl-stained rabbit brain image patches for three-class classification of photothrombotic lesions, necrotic regions, and normal tissue.
